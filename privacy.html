Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

n8n Personal Automation is a private application used only by its owner, Ivan Chavarro, to organize his own Gmail inbox. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the application reads messages in the owner's Gmail account, adds labels to them, and appends rows to a Google Sheet that the owner controls.

How the data is used

Email content is processed only to classify messages (for example payments or investments) and to record basic details such as date, amount, currency and subject in the owner's spreadsheet. Classification runs on an AI model hosted on the owner's own network.

Storage and sharing

  • Data stays within the owner's Google account and the owner's self-hosted n8n server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data from any other person's Google account is accessed.

Google API Services

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.
