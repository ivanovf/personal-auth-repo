Private, single-user app

Inbox, sorted on its own.

n8n Personal Automation is a self-hosted workflow that reads my own Gmail inbox, classifies each message with a local AI model, and logs payments and investments in my own Google Sheet. It is not a public service and has no other users.

01ReadNew emails arrive from my Gmail account.
02ClassifyA model on my own network labels each one.
03OrganizePayments and investments go to my spreadsheet.
